Meet Linda Jimeian

Raised Here. Ready to Serve.

Linda grew up in Indian Harbour Beach and Satellite Beach, attending Ocean Breeze Elementary and Satellite High School before graduating from Florida State University. This community shaped her values, and it has always been home.

After a successful career in business development, marketing, and community engagement, Linda returned home in 2001 to give back to the community she loves. In 2010, she began serving at Patrick Space Force Base, where she built strategic partnerships between local businesses and the military community.

Her career has been defined by bringing people together, strengthening organizations, and creating opportunities that improve the lives of individuals and families across Brevard County. Today, Linda continues that commitment as a member of the Indian Harbour Beach Planning and Zoning Board, where she works to support thoughtful planning, responsible growth, and decisions that preserve the character of our city.

Why I'm Running

Indian Harbour Beach has given so much to me throughout my life. I am running for City Council because I believe it is time to give back by serving our residents with integrity, transparency, and accountability.

I believe City Council should listen first, make decisions based on facts, protect taxpayer dollars, and always put the interests of our residents first. My goal is to preserve the hometown values we cherish while thoughtfully planning for the future of our community.

I want every resident to know that their voice matters. I will be accessible, approachable, and committed to working collaboratively to make decisions that strengthen our city today and for generations to come.
