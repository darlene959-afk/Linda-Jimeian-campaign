Meet Linda Jimeian

Linda Jimeian is a dedicated community leader with a passion for public service and building strong, connected communities. Throughout her career, she has worked across the nonprofit, business, and public sectors, bringing people together to solve problems, build partnerships, and improve the lives of others.

Linda's professional experience includes community engagement, workforce development, business development, fundraising, marketing, and case management. She has collaborated with local businesses, government agencies, faith-based organizations, and community partners to develop innovative solutions, secure resources, and strengthen community programs. Her work has focused on helping individuals and families achieve stability while supporting initiatives that enhance the quality of life for the entire community.

Committed to servant leadership, Linda currently serves on the Planning and Zoning Board, where she works to promote thoughtful growth, responsible planning, and decisions that protect the character of the community while preparing for its future.

Linda believes effective leadership begins with listening. She is committed to transparency, fiscal responsibility, public safety, and supporting local businesses while ensuring residents have a strong voice in the decisions that shape their city.

Linda is honored to seek the opportunity to serve on the City Council and is dedicated to working collaboratively to build a safe, vibrant, and thriving community for all residents.
